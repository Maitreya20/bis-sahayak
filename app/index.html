Is IS 302 mandatory for home appliances?
Yes — household appliances fall under IS 302 (Part 1) and need CRS registration before sale in India. Clause 4.2 covers protection against electric shock.

IS 302 (Part 1)
Safety of household and similar electrical appliances · General requirements · Clause 4.2
✅ Verified from BIS source
Clause 4.2 — Protection against electric shock Protection shall be provided against electric shock even in the case of damage to the appliance, such as breakage of a component, deterioration of insulation or deposit of dust… accessible live parts shall not be present under single fault conditions.
Related clause — Marking (8.1) The appliance shall be marked with the manufacturer's name or trade mark, the rated voltage, and the IS number where CRS applies.
