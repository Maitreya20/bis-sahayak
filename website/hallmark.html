The four signs of a genuine hallmark

IS 1417 requires all four on every gold article (§ Clause 5)

SIGN 01BIS logoThe Bureau of Indian Standards mark.
SIGN 02Purity grade916 = 22K, 750 = 18K, parts per thousand.
SIGN 03Assay centre markIdentifier of the centre that tested it.
SIGN 04HUIDUnique ID — the one you verify here.
